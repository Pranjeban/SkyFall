## 8. Dependency verification (Researcher, official sources)

**Evidence status:** code-visible facts were independently verified by the Architect **[V]**. The external registry/documentation claims below are high-credibility researcher findings but were **not independently reproduced** by the Architect; treat them as **[A — confirm before Phase 6 acts]**.
