## Summary
Most v1.1 modules are missing or partial relative to the current v0.9.2 codebase. The largest gaps: Azure monitoring/cost/security, Power Apps, Solutions, cross-platform intelligence, write operations, and the controlled execution runner (gated in v1.0 preview). Full v1.1 delivery requires phased implementation; this baseline captures the gap.
